**HCI Lab (Human-Computer Interaction Lab)** has launched a new research project, **"CORAL: Development of Co-Regulation Agent Loop Technology for Daily Rhythm Recovery in Bipolar Disorder"**, supported by the **National Research Foundation of Korea (NRF)**.

We look forward to their impactful contributions and wish the HCI Lab research team great success with the new project! 🎉
