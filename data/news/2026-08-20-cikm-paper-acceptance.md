Two research papers from **DM Lab (Data Mining Lab)** have been accepted to **ACM CIKM 2026** (Short Paper Track) [BKCSA 3].

- **PRISM: Reliable LLM Knowledge Distillation from Graph-Context Perspectives for Text-Attributed Graph Learning**  
  *Junghyun Kim, Sobin Jung, David Y. Kang, Sang-Wook Kim*
- **Who Should Teach? Confidence-Aware Dual-Teacher Learning for Few-Shot Node Classification on Text-Attributed Graphs**  
  *Hojin Kim, Sujin Yoon, Sungsu Lim, Dongwon Lee, David Y. Kang*

Congratulations to the authors and the DM Lab research team on this outstanding achievement! 🎉
