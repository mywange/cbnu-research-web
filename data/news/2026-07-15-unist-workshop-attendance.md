Members of **CBNU DxH Research Group (DM Lab & HCI Lab)** participated in the **1st Data Intelligence × Human-Computer Interaction Workshop** hosted at UNIST (Ulsan National Institute of Science and Technology).

Faculty members and students from both labs shared their latest research findings and engaged in fruitful discussions on interdisciplinary research bridging data mining algorithms and interactive intelligent systems.
