Who We Are

CBNU Research Group

The CBNU Research Group is a collaborative research initiative uniting the Human-Computer Interaction (HCI) Lab and the Data Mining (DM) Lab from the School of Information and Communication Engineering and the School of Computer Science at Chungbuk National University. We investigate the principles governing interactions between humans and intelligent systems, researching and developing technologies that extract meaningful value and insights from large-scale, complex data.

Our core research areas span Human-AI Interaction, Graph Mining & LLMs, Computing for Well-being, Text Mining & LLMs, AI for Healthcare, Embodied Interaction in XR, and Behavioral Sensing & Modeling. Grounded in rigorous empirical methodologies, we consistently publish our findings in premier international conferences and journals, including ACM CHI, ACM KDD, ACM CIKM, UbiComp, WWW, and IEEE TKDE.

Our Vision

Empowering Human Agency, Engineering Data Intelligence

We envision a future where artificial intelligence does not merely replace humans, but enhances human agency and provides genuine utility. By combining a deep understanding of user context and behavior with trustworthy, user-centered AI interactions and scalable data mining technologies, we pursue practical research that delivers positive real-world impact.

Our Mission

Discover, Design, Disseminate

We discover new insights from human behavior and complex data (Discover), design intelligent systems based on these findings (Design), and disseminate validated research outcomes across academia and industry (Disseminate).

Discover

Uncovering latent human needs and structural patterns within complex data through systematic empirical research and multimodal sensing.

Design

Designing and implementing systems for real-world problem-solving by coupling human-centered principles with scalable graph AI algorithms.

Disseminate

Broadening the societal impact of our research through top-tier conference publications, open-source releases, and industry-academic partnerships.
