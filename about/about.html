Who We Are

CBNU DxH Research Group

The CBNU DxH Research Group is a collaborative research initiative uniting the Data Mining (DM) Lab and the Human-Computer Interaction (HCI) Lab from the School of Computer Science and the School of Information and Communication Engineering at Chungbuk National University. We investigate the principles governing interactions between humans and intelligent systems, researching and developing technologies that extract meaningful value and insights from large-scale, complex data.

Our core research areas span Graph Mining & LLMs, Human-AI Interaction, Text Mining & LLMs, Computing for Well-being, AI for Healthcare, Embodied Interaction in XR, and Behavioral Sensing & Modeling. Grounded in rigorous empirical methodologies, we consistently publish our findings in premier international conferences and journals, including ACM KDD, ACM CHI, ACM CIKM, UbiComp, WWW, and IEEE TKDE.
